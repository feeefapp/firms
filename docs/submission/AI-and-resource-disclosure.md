# AI assistance and external resource credits

Prepared for the Spacedev team's NASA Space Apps 2026 submission, **Firms: One Comparable Fire History**.

## AI / SI assistance

Claude and OpenAI Codex assisted with software implementation, debugging, research, interpretation of analysis outputs, explanatory writing, visual layout, and preparation of the presentation and submission text. The web application also contains an optional language-model assistant connected to project data through tools and MCP.

Code, text, and presentation layout are AI-assisted. The seven-slide PDF carries a visible disclosure and identifies the assistance in its metadata. AI assistance does not mean NASA verified the code, interpretations, or project.

Scientific fire observations are sourced from NASA FIRMS, not generated by AI. Charts are computed from observations or explicitly labelled as illustrative examples. NASA Earth textures are sourced imagery, not AI-generated imagery. Demo screenshots reproduce the implemented application; no synthetic satellite measurements are presented as observations.

Tools: [Claude](https://claude.ai/), [OpenAI Codex](https://openai.com/codex/).

## NASA observations and imagery

- [NASA FIRMS](https://earthdata.nasa.gov/firms): MODIS and VIIRS active fire detections, including the historical archive and recent API observations. We acknowledge NASA FIRMS, part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).
- [FIRMS archive and API](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/): observation access and documentation.
- [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/): cloud information used when evaluating a cloud-correction approach; the selected ensemble does not apply that correction.
- [NASA Blue Marble](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/collections/blue-marble/) and [nighttime Earth imagery](https://blackmarble.gsfc.nasa.gov/): globe textures, distributed through the [three-globe example image set, version 2.31.0](https://unpkg.com/three-globe@2.31.0/example/img/).
- The presentation's globe uses an archived sample of 6,000 real MODIS/VIIRS observations from 1 November 2026. It is not a complete global count or a live feed. See [presentation asset credits](../../web/public/presentation/README.md).

## Other observations and geographic resources

- NOAA GOES active fire observations through FIRMS were used to examine the daily fire cycle and an observation-time correction. This correction was not retained in the selected method.
- [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org/): satellite orbital elements used for orbit visualization.
- [Natural Earth via world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) and [topojson-client](https://github.com/topojson/topojson-client): geographic outlines and topology decoding.
- [Esri World Imagery](https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/data-attributions): zoom-dependent satellite imagery tiles in the globe; retain the source attribution.
